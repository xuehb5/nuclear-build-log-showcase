# Nuclear Project Map Guide: From Province to Site and Unit

**[English](map-guide.en.md) · [简体中文](map-guide.md) · [Product overview](../README.en.md)**

These captures come from the actual running website on 29 October 2026. Selection and browsing do not modify project records. Stages and counts represent the ledger at the time of capture.

## Step 1: Click a province on the map

Click the Guangdong provincial area itself. The province is highlighted and markers appear for Lianjiang, Lufeng, Taipingling and Taishan. The right panel switches to Guangdong nuclear projects. This example contains four projects and 16 recorded units; the panel can be scrolled to inspect the remaining records.

![Actual Guangdong selection and project list](../assets/nuclear-map.png)

Projects list their companies and unit codes, with reactor types and current stages for individual units. Stage colours follow the progress visualization, connecting the geographic distribution with the engineering records.

## Step 2: Select a site marker or project name

Click the Lufeng marker or the Lufeng project name in the right panel. The map displays only the selected site's marker with a Lufeng label, while the corresponding project card is highlighted. Other projects in the province remain in the panel for comparison and further selection.

![Actual Lufeng selection with a single site marker](../assets/nuclear-map-site.png)

Selection works in both directions: start with a province and choose a marker, or select a project name to locate its site. Clicking a province again restores the display of its project markers.

## Step 3: Open a unit's project record

Click a specific unit code, such as LUFENG-5, to open its project-status search and inspect milestone dates, company and reactor type.

![Existing Lufeng project-status workspace capture](../assets/project-status.png)

The map does not independently infer unit stages. Valid updates from confirmed news, using AI-assisted interpretation and software checks, or from manual maintenance feed the same project ledger read by the map and progress chart. Selecting a province or site is a viewing action and does not itself update progress.
